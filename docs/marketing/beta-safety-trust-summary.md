# Styx Beta Safety & Trust Summary

Styx is engineered from the ground up around user trust, financial safety, and privacy. Here is a summary of our core safety mechanisms for Beta participants:

## 1. Financial Safety & FBO Escrow
- **No Proprietary Commingling**: All user stakes are held off-balance-sheet in Stripe For-Benefit-Of (FBO) escrow accounts.
- **Idempotent Settlement**: Real-money payouts and contract resolutions are governed by automated, double-pay-proof settlement workers.

## 2. Privacy & Data Minimization
- **Strict PII Redaction**: Personal identifiers and contact information are automatically redacted before storage or logging.
- **Biometric Classification**: Passive health data (e.g. step counts, wearable logs) is strictly segregated from biometric identifiers, complying with BIPA, CUBI, and MHMDA frameworks.

## 3. Aegis Safety Protocol & Crisis Safeguards
- **Velocity Caps**: Contract stake parameters enforce a 2% weekly limit to prevent unsafe over-commitment.
- **Automated Crisis Interventions**: Built-in content analysis automatically flags distress indicators and provides immediate support resources.

## 4. Peer Audit Integrity
- **Double-Blind Auditing**: Peer verifiers evaluate anonymized behavioral proofs without access to user identities.
- **Triadic Consensus**: Dispute resolutions require multi-agent consensus, eliminating individual bias.
