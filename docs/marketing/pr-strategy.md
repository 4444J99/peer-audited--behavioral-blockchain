# PR Strategy — App Store Launch
> Issue: #346
> Phase: Pre-Launch (T-30 days → Launch +14)

## Overview

Styx faces a unique PR challenge: it's a financial accountability platform that looks like gambling to a casual observer. The narrative must pre-empt that objection while leaning into the genuinely novel value: peer-audited behavioral contracts with real escrowed money and loss-aversion physics. This strategy positions Styx as behavioral science innovation, not a betting app.

## Narrative Angles

### Primary Angle: "The App That Finally Makes Accountability Work"

**Hook:** "What if you had to put money on the line to keep your New Year's resolution — and a jury of strangers verified the result?"

**Key messages:**
- Styx closes the $10B gap between intention and action
- Combines behavioral economics (loss aversion λ=1.955) with financial escrow (Stripe FBO)
- Peer audit network ("Fury") eliminates self-reporting fraud
- First 500 beta users averaged [X]% completion rate vs. industry 25-40%

### Secondary Angle: "The Science of Follow-Through"

**Hook:** "92% of goals fail. What if the answer isn't more willpower, but better incentives?"

**Key messages:**
- Grounded in Kahneman & Tversky's Prospect Theory
- Real financial stakes + peer verification = 2-3x completion rate
- Built on a double-entry ledger (no phantom money, full audit trail)
- Created by a team with background in behavioral economics + fintech

### Tertiary Angle: "Accountability Tech for Recovery"

**Hook:** "One of the most painful experiences in modern life — breakups, addiction recovery — has a surprisingly effective tool: money."

**Key messages:**
- Recovery contracts help people maintain no-contact, sobriety, boundaries
- Aegis Protocol provides health guardrails (BMI floors, velocity caps)
- Practitioners (therapists, coaches) prescribe contracts as clinical tool
- Privacy firewalled: employer/practitioner sees aggregate data only

## Crisis PR Preparedness

| Scenario | Response Plan |
|----------|---------------|
| "Styx is gambling" viral thread | Pre-drafted response: Distinction between skill-based contract (you control outcome) and gambling (random). Peer audit verifies actual behavior. Escrow returns your money on completion. |
| Accusation of profiting from addiction | Hold statement: Aegis Protocol health guardrails prevent dangerous contracts. Practitioners oversee clinical use. Revenue is from platform fees, not user failure. |
| Data breach or security incident | Full incident response protocol (see docs/operations/incident-response.md). PR: Transparency, timeline, user protections. |
| Stripe dispute / payment issue | Proactive: Announce before Stripe flags. Explain reconciliation process, user protections. |
| Negative app store review campaign | Monitor, respond professionally, flag to Apple if coordinated. |
| Regulatory inquiry | Legal counsel review. "We welcome the conversation — accountability tech needs clear rules." |
