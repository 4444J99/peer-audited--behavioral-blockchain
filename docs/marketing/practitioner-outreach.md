### One-Pager Content Draft

**Problem:** Your clients show up for sessions. They're motivated. They make commitments. Then real life hits, and the between-session follow-through collapses. Research shows homework compliance hovers around 40% — not because clients don't want to change, but because willpower is a depletable resource.

**Solution:** Styx is the only platform that enforces client accountability through real financial stakes and peer-audited proof. Think of it as a commitment device your clients can't back out of.

**How it works:**
1. You assign a contract (or client creates their own)
2. Client stakes money via Stripe escrow (you choose the amount)
3. Client submits proof of completion (photo, video, screenshot)
4. Peer auditors (Fury Network) verify the proof
5. Client gets money back on success; forfeits on failure

**Why practitioners choose Styx:**
- **Better outcomes:** Financial stakes increase completion rates 2-3x vs. willpower alone
- **Privacy first:** We never store health data; you see aggregate completion metrics, not individual proof
- **Evidence-based:** Grounded in Prospect Theory (Kahneman & Tversky), loss aversion coefficient λ=1.955
- **Professional tool:** Dashboard tracks client progress, contract templates, analytics

**Free trial:** 30 days, up to 3 clients. No credit card required.

### Demo Call Script

**Duration:** 15 minutes.

| Section | Time | Content |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Opener** | 2 min | "Thanks for the time. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this tight. Quick question to start: what's the biggest challenge you see with clients following through between sessions?" |
| **Problem confirmation** | 3 min | "So if I told you there was a way to increase follow-through by 2-3x using behavioral economics, would that be useful?" |
| **Demo** | 5 min | Screen share: Create a contract → set stake → client view → proof upload → audit result → completion. Show practitioner dashboard (completion rates, client progress). |
| **Pricing** | 2 min | "Free for 30 days, 3 clients. Solo tier is $49/mo after that — but beta participants lock in at $29/mo for life." |

### Objection Response Guide

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "This sounds like gambling." | "I understand why it looks that way. The key difference: the outcome is 100% controlled by the client's behavior. Gambling is random chance. Styx is a commitment contract — like a gym membership fee, but you get it back if you go." |
| "My clients won't put money on the line." | "That's what I thought too — then our beta showed 65%+ of users choose to stake. The key is framing: it's not a bet, it's a deposit. You get it back when you follow through. Most people want that external lock-in." |
| "I don't have time to learn another tool." | "Setup takes 10 minutes. You can assign a contract in 30 seconds. The dashboard updates automatically. I'll personally walk you through it." |
| "What about privacy?" | "We're a financial platform with a privacy firewall. We never store health data. Practitioners see only aggregate completion stats, not individual proof content." |
| "Is it expensive?" | "Free for 30 days. $49/mo after — less than one client session. If it improves outcomes for even one client, it pays for itself." |
| "Does it actually work?" | "Our beta shows [X]% completion rate across [Y] contracts. The research on loss aversion (Kahneman & Tversky, 1979; Gneezy & Rustichini, 2000) consistently shows 2-3x improvement in goal adherence with financial stakes." |
