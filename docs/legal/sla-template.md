# Enterprise Service Level Agreement (SLA) Template

**Effective Date:** March 10, 2026  
**Provider:** Styx / ORGANVM

## 1. Service Availability Commitment
Styx commits to maintaining a **99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage** for API endpoints, core contract settlement workers, and enterprise management dashboards.

## 2. Support Response Levels

| Severity Level | Description | Target First Response | Target Resolution / Workaround |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1 - Critical** | Complete service outage affecting contract settlement or payment processing | < 1 hour | < 4 hours |
| **P2 - High** | Major feature impairment without immediate workaround | < 4 hours | < 24 hours |
| **P3 - Normal** | Minor issue or general enterprise support request | < 1 business day | < 3 business days |

## 3. SLA Service Credits
If Monthly Uptime falls below 99.9%, Enterprise Client is eligible for a credit applied against future subscription fees:
- **< 99.9% to 99.0%**: 10% monthly service credit
- **< 99.0% to 95.0%**: 25% monthly service credit
- **< 95.0%**: 50% monthly service credit
