## The Problem

Behavioral change fails because accountability is cheap. Verbal commitments evaporate. Therapist sessions create insight but not durable skin-in-the-game. Your clients know what to do. They don't have a mechanism that makes *not doing it* costly enough.

---

## What Styx Does

Styx turns behavioral commitments into financially-staked, cryptographically-verified contracts audited by an anonymous peer network.

**The mechanism:**
1. **Client stakes money** on a behavioral oath (e.g., No-Contact for 30 days). Loss aversion (λ = 1.955) means a $50 stake feels like $97.75 of motivation.
2. **Daily attestations** are timestamped and hashed. Client submits video proof statements that can't be faked retroactively.
3. **Fury Network** — 2-of-3 anonymous peer auditors review submitted proofs and vote on compliance. No single auditor can manipulate outcomes; collusion triggers automatic stake slashing.
4. **Settlement** — Client gets their stake back on success. On failure, stake is slashed to a configured destination (your practice fund, a charity, or the Styx system).
