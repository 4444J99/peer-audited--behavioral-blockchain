# Jessica presenter script

Every spoken claim below carries a truth label. Keep the label in the sentence; it is part of the product story.

## Opening — 30 seconds

“This is a private Styx demonstration using fictional users and test credits. **Working today:** a person can make a commitment, submit a proof record, and inspect the result. **Test-money beta:** the next external step is consented and hosted, but it is not real money. **Future enterprise capability:** the employer view is directionally useful, while procurement and operational controls remain gates.”

Open `/tour` and pause at the accounting-boundary card.

## Demo 1 — individual commitment, proof, and auditable result — 2 minutes

1. Sign in as `river@demo.styx.protocol` with the synthetic demo password shown in the local runbook.
2. Open the dashboard, then a commitment and its proof record.
3. Show the check-in history and the test-credit ledger entry.

Say: “**Working today:** this is a linked record of the commitment, evidence, review, and test-credit entry. The label matters: no card was charged and no real balance exists. **Test-money beta:** we would repeat this only after the hosted verification gate passes.”

## Demo 2 — coach assignment and review — 2 minutes

1. Sign in as `dr.moira@demo.styx.protocol`.
2. Open `/practitioner` and the assigned-client view.
3. Point to commitment status and agreed alerts; do not open or narrate source material beyond the prepared synthetic screen.

Say: “**Working today:** assignment access is scoped in the synthetic demo. The point is accountability context, not surveillance. **Test-money beta:** a real coach pilot needs consent, support operations, and a clear minimum-data agreement. This is not a clinical workflow or a diagnosis.”

## Demo 3 — enterprise preview — 90 seconds

1. Sign in as `hr.lead@acheron.example`.
2. Open `/hr` and the aggregate program preview.
3. Show the privacy/tenant boundary rather than individual source records.

Say: “**Future enterprise capability:** this view shows what aggregate oversight could look like using synthetic data. It is not a procurement claim. Hosting, geography, consent operations, security review, and buyer approval are deliberate gates before any employer deployment.”

## Close — 20 seconds

“The red bow is not a promise to skip safeguards. It is a runnable, inspectable demo now; a safe test-money beta when its gates are proven; and an enterprise direction that remains explicitly later.”
