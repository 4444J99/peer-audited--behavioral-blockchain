# Coach-first test-money pilot kit

## Positioning

Styx is a private accountability demonstration for independent executive and life coaches who want a participant-owned record of a commitment, check-ins, and a reviewable test-credit outcome. It is not a clinical platform, a payments product, or a public enterprise offer.

## Safe pilot offer — Test-money beta

Offer a small, consented, hosted test-money cohort only after the launch-gates receipt is green. The offer contains no published price, no real-money settlement, and no clinical or performance-outcome promise. The pilot conversation should establish:

- a coach-selected, non-clinical accountability use case;
- what the participant agrees the coach may see;
- a minimal-data approach and support path;
- the explicit test-money-only boundary; and
- the exit and feedback process.

## Discovery-call script

“I am showing a synthetic demo of a commitment and accountability record. **Working today:** you can see the participant, coach, and aggregate views in test data. **Test-money beta:** we are considering a small consented cohort after hosting, consent, support, geography, and verification gates are proven. What accountability moment is difficult to support today, and what is the minimum signal that would be useful without creating a surveillance relationship?”

Follow-ups:

- “Who would choose to participate, and how would they understand the boundary?”
- “What must remain private from the coach?”
- “What would make the pilot safe to stop?”
- “What evidence would tell you that the workflow is worth continuing to evaluate?”

Do not quote pricing, predicted retention, clinical outcomes, market size, or real-money availability.

## Demo follow-up

Subject: `Private Styx test-money demo — boundaries and next step`

“Thank you for reviewing the synthetic Styx demo. The current product story is intentionally narrow: a test-credit accountability record, scoped coach review, and an enterprise-direction preview. No real money, clinical service, pricing, or employer deployment is being offered. If the fit is worth exploring, the next step is to define a minimal-data, consented test-money pilot once the documented hosting and verification gates are passed.”

| Window | Action | Guardrail |
